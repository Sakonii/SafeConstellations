**Official Comment: Overall Feedback**

The paper received overall scores of 3.5 (xCj3), 3.5 (Zici), and 2 (JwzU), with corresponding soundness scores of 4, 4, and 3. All reviewers acknowledged the conceptual importance of distinguishing erroneous over-refusal from appropriate harmful refusal. Reviewer xCj3 noted that the geometric characterization of over-refusal is insightful and well supported through multiple complementary analyses rather than a single probe or visualization, and emphasized the clear practical implications for refusal-ablation methods. Reviewer Zici highlighted the combination of confirmatory and novel empirical findings, the paper's grounding in the existing literature, and the careful consideration of alternative hypotheses throughout the step-by-step analysis. Reviewer JwzU recognized the importance and timeliness of the problem and identified the observation that over-refusal instances remain embedded within task-specific clusters, rather than collapsing into a shared refusal cluster, as a useful insight.

We thank all the reviewers for their constructive feedback. During the rebuttal period, we addressed the main concerns raised: we evaluated seven new task frames; partially replicated the central directional analysis on a second model family (Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct); independently validated the refusal annotations using a second judge from a different model family; and added further controls for prompt templates, content variation, and statistical robustness.
